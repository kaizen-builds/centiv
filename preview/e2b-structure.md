# E2B structure notes (reference only)

Studied 12 Nov 2026 from https://designdetails.io/websites/e2b-dev and https://e2b.dev.
Reference shots: /workspace/centiv-video/stills/e2b-ref-*.png

## What Design Details lists
Navbar, split hero with product panel, two-row logo strip, feature collection,
use-case gallery, stats, integration grid, pricing, footer.
Style: brutalist, typographic, minimal, dark-mode native.
Type (live site): display PP Supply Sans ~72px / 56px, body Inter 16/24,
mono labels in brackets. Colours on e2b.dev: black ground, near-white type,
orange #E32A01 buttons. Motion: short hovers, product-panel state, little
scroll choreography.

## Live e2b.dev chapter order (desktop, measured)
1. Short nav (few destinations, sign-in, one primary button)
2. Split hero: bracketed label, specific oversized headline, short body,
   filled + outline CTAs, product/dashboard panel on the right
3. Logo strip under a one-line label (“100K+ teams…”)
4. Typographic thesis chapter, lots of empty space, optional diagram
5. Use-case card carousel
6. Feature bento with terminal/network diagrams
7. Deployment / isolation chapters
8. Marquee / stats, testimonials
9. Integration logo grid under a huge headline
10. Pricing table, closing CTA, column footer

## What Centiv borrows (structure and feel only)
Short nav. Split hero with a log-style product panel. Labeled partner strip.
Oversized chapter headlines and generous vertical space. A three-up feature
grid. A closing typographic CTA and a column footer. Staggered line-print
motion in the log.

## What Centiv does not take
E2B copy, logos, assets, black/orange palette, all-caps condensed display,
sharp-corner buttons, bracketed “[ LABEL ]” voice, pixel-noise textures,
or Tranche’s look. Brand stays cream paper, plum #5B2A4E, DM Sans +
Newsreader italic, rounded plum pills, gold cards, white passkey card.
Always “Kite Passport”.
